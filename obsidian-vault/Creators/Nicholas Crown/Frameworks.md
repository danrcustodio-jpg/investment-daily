# Frameworks

Methods from last-month public posts. Not entries/stops/sizes.

1. **Teach the mechanism, sell the blotter** — fact → Comment LETTER. Steal the question.
2. **Trade the spread, not the headline** — cracks vs crude, tanks vs paper oil.
3. **Positioning confirms a break** — CoT/CTAs after the gold break, not instead of price.
4. **Rates discount everything** — bad-news rallies via the Fed path; gold opportunity cost.
5. **Some rallies are paid for** — Japan intervention, war talk-down, bond buyers. Who funds it?
6. **AI bottleneck is power** — not crude-as-fuel; memory squeeze vs Apple is the chip side.
7. **Options are a job** — view, speed, isolate risk. 0DTE as crowding/hedge, not lottery.
8. **Stops are liquidity** — ATR past round numbers.
9. **Breadth so you do not invent trades** — no setup, no trade. Same day as “village drunk” tape: [[Creators/Nicholas Crown/TikTok/2026-08-20 earnings priced wander]].
10. **Policy playbooks repeat** — Kalshi/NY tax pattern.
11. **VIX/10 as a noise filter** — [[Creators/Nicholas Crown/TikTok/2026-08-20 VIX over 10]].
12. **Who polices the bond market?** — Fed vs Treasury divergence → gold as the unofficial cop.
