# Glossary

Plain English. Then what it does **not** prove. Mix of [[Creators/Nicholas Crown/TikTok/Index|TikTok]] (Aug 19–20) and [[Creators/Nicholas Crown/LinkedIn month]].

## VIX / 10

Divide the **VIX by 10** to get a rough **S&P daily expected move in percent** (VIX 16 → ~1.6%). From [[Creators/Nicholas Crown/TikTok/2026-08-20 VIX over 10]].

Does not prove: tomorrow will move that much. It is implied vol, not a crystal ball.

## Fed vs Treasury / who polices bonds

When **fiscal (Treasury)** and **monetary (Fed)** look uncoordinated, **risk is hard to price**; he says **gold** benefits if nobody is clearly **policing the long end**. See [[Creators/Nicholas Crown/TikTok/2026-08-19 Fed vs Treasury]] and [[Creators/Nicholas Crown/TikTok/2026-08-20 long end gold]].

Does not prove: always long gold when they disagree.

## 0DTE

Options that expire **the same day**. Crowding → **dealer hedges in futures** can move the S&P more than the premium.

Does not prove: you should trade 0DTE.

## Dealer / market-maker hedge

Dealers **delta-hedge** inventory with futures/shares. Size can **push, pin, or accelerate**.

Does not prove: manipulation for sport.

## Gamma / pin

Hedges can **pin** near popular strikes. This month’s public captions did not republish old GEX level numbers.

## Comment LETTER / Crown Macro

Free clip = mechanism. Paid letter = blotter. Not a verified track record.

## CoT (Commitments of Traders)

CFTC futures positioning. He used it **after** a gold break (fresh longs, CTAs). Lagged; can be crowded.

## CTA

Trend-followers. They **chase breaks** and can dump if trend fails.

## Crack spread (3:2:1)

~2 gasoline + 1 diesel − 3 crude, ÷3 = refiner margin per barrel. “42” converts $/gal to $/bbl. He said cracks ~$70, ~3× history.

Does not prove: buy refiners blindly.

## Tank bottom

Storage so empty pumps struggle (~21M vs ~76M, “since 2014”). Headline dumps can still meet **physical** buyers.

## War premium / manufactured rally

Bids from intervention / jawboning **last until the payer stops**.

## Bond vigilantes

Sellers who force **yields up** and can cap equities.

## Opportunity cost (gold)

Gold yield ~0. **Lower expected rates** make gold less painful vs cash.

## Gold as chameleon

Gold wears **whatever is driving the tape** — not one job.

## Breadth / opportunity surface

Few indicators on few tickers → forced trades. Watch **pairs, calendars, breadth, funding**.

## Relative value / calendar spread

Long one, short the other (or two dates). His old **futures RV** job.

## ATR stop buffer past round numbers

Stops cluster at round numbers (~5×, NY Fed staff paper cited). Stop **a slice of ATR beyond**. Heuristic, not a system.

## Options job-matching

Before the chain: view, **speed**, **which risk to isolate**.

## Rotation (not buy small caps)

A mapped rotation ≠ lazy IWM.

## AI → power stack (not crude)

Data centers run on **grid**, not barrels (~0.7% of utility generation from oil). Milk story = **electricity in CPI**.
