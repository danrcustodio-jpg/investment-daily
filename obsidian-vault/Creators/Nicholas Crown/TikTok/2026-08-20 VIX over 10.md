# VIX divided by 10

**Source:** TikTok Latest, opened 2026-08-20. Snowflake UTC ~15:09.

## What he said

**VIX / 10 ≈ expected S&P daily move in percent.** Example: VIX **16** → about **1.6%** as a “normal” day. Use it to tell **noise** from a move that actually matters.

## So what

This is a **rule of thumb**, not a forecast. Implied vol is a **priced distribution**, not tomorrow’s print. Still a useful filter next to [[Creators/Nicholas Crown/TikTok/2026-08-20 earnings priced wander]].
