# Glossary (terms from the 2026-07-20 – 2026-08-20 public posts)

Rolled up from [month-notes.md](month-notes.md) and four TikToks opened in a browser 2026-08-19–20. Plain English, then what it does **not** prove.

## VIX / 10

**VIX ÷ 10 ≈ expected S&P daily move in percent** (VIX 16 → ~1.6%). Noise vs signal. Implied vol, not a forecast of tomorrow.

## Fed vs Treasury (who polices bonds)

If fiscal and monetary policy **diverge**, risk is hard to price; he says **gold** wins when it is unclear who is **policing the long end**.

## 0DTE

Options that expire **the same day**. Tiny index moves can nuke or 10x the premium. Crown’s point this month: crowding at strikes forces **market-maker hedges in futures**, so the **hedge** can move the S&amp;P more than the option premium suggests.

Does not prove: you should trade 0DTE, or that a pin will hold tomorrow.

## Dealer / market-maker hedge

When customers buy (or sell) options, dealers often take the other side and **delta-hedge** with futures or shares. Enough size can **push, pin, or accelerate** the index near popular strikes.

Does not prove: dealers are “manipulating” for sport. It is inventory management.

## Gamma / pin

In older Crown posts (outside this window) he talks **positive gamma cages**. This month’s 0DTE clip is the same family: hedges can **pin** price near a strike. This window’s public captions did **not** republish the 7000–7025 GEX numbers; do not invent them.

## Comment LETTER / Crown Macro

Call to action for the **paid weekly letter**. Free post = mechanism. Paid product = claimed setups and blotter.

Does not prove: the letter’s historical accuracy.

## CoT (Commitments of Traders)

CFTC report of **how large specs, commercials, etc. are positioned** in futures. He used it this month as **confirmation after a gold “break”**: fresh longs + “CTAs rush in.”

Does not prove: CoT is a timing tool. It is lagged and crowded itself.

## CTA

Trend-following funds. “CTAs rush in” means **systematic buying after a break**, which can extend a move — and also dump if the trend fails.

## Crack spread (3:2:1)

Street shorthand for **refiner margin**: roughly **2 gasoline + 1 diesel − 3 crude**, then divide by 3 (per barrel). The “42” converts **$/gallon products** to **$/barrel**. This month he said front-month cracks were **~triple history** (~$70/bbl in the caption): **products ripping, crude lagging**.

Does not prove: buy refiners blindly. Cracks mean-revert; geopolitics and diesel vs gasoline matter.

## Tank bottom

Physical **crude storage so empty** that pumps struggle (he cited ~21M vs ~76M capacity, “lowest since 2014”). Claim: **headline-driven dumps still meet physical buyers**.

Does not prove: WTI must rally tomorrow. Empty tanks are a *physical* argument, not a chart signal.

## War premium / manufactured rally

Oil (and risk assets) priced for **conflict / intervention / official jawboning**. He said rallies **die when Japan stops intervening, “war talk-down” stops working, or bond vigilantes sell**.

Does not prove: which of those three hits first.

## Bond vigilantes

Bond sellers who **force yields higher** when they think fiscal/inflation policy is unsustainable. Higher yields can **cap equities**.

## Opportunity cost (gold)

Gold’s “yield” is ~0. If **expected future rates fall**, holding gold is **less painful** vs cash/bonds — one reason **bad news** (growth scare → easier Fed) can lift gold **and** stocks.

Does not prove: gold is only a rates trade. Same month he called gold a **chameleon**.

## Gold as chameleon

Do not assign gold **one job** (only inflation hedge, only fear). It **takes the identity of whatever is driving the tape** (rates, USD, positioning, geopolitics).

## Breadth / opportunity surface

His process pitch: **few indicators on few tickers** → almost no real trades → people **force** trades. Fix: watch **many relative-value surfaces** (cross-asset pairs, calendar spreads, breadth ratios, funding) so you wait for something that is **actually live**.

Does not prove: more screens = more edge. More surfaces also means more noise.

## Relative value / calendar spread

**Long one, short the other** (or two dates on the same contract) so you trade the **spread**, not a naked direction. Matches his old **futures RV** job.

## ATR stop buffer past round numbers

Claim: stops **cluster at round numbers** (~5× density, citing a **NY Fed staff report**). Price **sweeps** through, triggers stops, then reverses. Tactic: put the stop **a fraction of ATR beyond** the round number.

Does not prove: the “unbelievable results” line. It is a **placement heuristic**, not a system.

## Options job-matching

Before the chain: (1) **what view**, (2) **how fast**, (3) **which risk to isolate**. Pick a structure that **matches the job** (not “buy calls because bullish”).

## Rotation (not “buy small caps”)

From out-of-window TikTok snippets and the same LETTER funnel: a **mapped rotation** is **not** a lazy IWM buy. This month’s LinkedIn tape was more **SPX resistance, energy products, AI power, gold** than a small-cap manifesto.

## AI → power stack (not crude)

Data centers run on **grid electricity**, not barrels. He cited oil as **~0.7%** of U.S. utility-scale generation. Bottleneck: **cheap power + delivery**. Milk-at-record / farmer-pay-cut story: **refrigeration electricity** as a **sticky CPI** channel.

Does not prove: which utility/nuclear/nat-gas ticker. That stays in the letter.

## SPCX / chip order flow

Title-only this month: **SPCX is changing chip order flow**. Caption too thin to define the ticker or mechanism here.

## Prediction-market tax playbook (Kalshi)

NY suing Kalshi framed as **ban vice → tax vice → sue the untaxable version** (alcohol, lotteries, sportsbooks). Trade idea is **downstream of legislation**, not “fade Kalshi on the app.”
