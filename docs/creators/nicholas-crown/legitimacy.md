## What the last-month feed actually is

Almost every substantive post ends with **Comment LETTER** or **link in profile**. Free clips are **top-of-funnel**: teach one mechanism, withhold the blotter (entry, stop, size, multi-leg structure).

Letter marketing claims a **1,700-signal database**, proprietary algos, “full trade structures.” Those systems are **not inspectable** from public captions. Follower count and “institutional-grade” wording are **not** a verified P&amp;L.

## How to use this without getting played

1. **Steal the question, not the ticker.** “Is this a crack-spread story or a crude-direction story?” is usable. “Click my profile for the trades” is a checkout.
2. **Separate three jobs:** (a) teaching market structure, (b) current macro *opinion* (gold flip, SPX resistance, empty tanks), (c) product (letter, Revise). (a) can be durable; (b) expires; (c) is sales.
3. **Do not score “was he right.”** This pass does not backtest his calls. Even a correct gold flip does not validate the paid package.
4. **Incentives:** he is paid if you subscribe and if Revise gathers AUM. That does not make every framework false; it means thin clips are optimized to **sound complete** while the executable part stays gated.

## Conflicts with this repo

Investment Daily scans **price/volume indicators** on a small ticker list. Crown’s last-month language is **cross-asset, positioning, and options structure**. Mapping his clips into `strategy_engine.py` would mix two different processes. Do not do that unless you later design a separate, tested module.
