# Frameworks (last-month public posts only)

These are **methods he repeated** in 2026-07-20 – 2026-08-20 captions. They are not entries, stops, or sizes. Paid blotter stays gated.

## 1. Teach the mechanism, sell the blotter

Pattern: one concrete market fact (crack formula, tank levels, 0DTE hedges, milk/power) → **Comment LETTER**.

How to listen: write down the **mechanism**. Ignore “how we’re positioned” unless you actually subscribe and can verify it yourself.

## 2. Trade the spread, not the headline

- Crude can dump on ceasefire talk while **products/cracks** scream shortage.
- Empty **tanks** can floor dips even if paper oil looks weak.
- **3:2:1 crack** as the refiner P&amp;L tape, not “oil is up/down.”

Same family as his old **futures relative-value** job: the money is often in **the relationship**.

## 3. Positioning confirms a break; it does not replace one

Gold example this month: **price break first**, then **CoT longs + CTA chase** as confirmation of **flow**, then CTA to subscribe for the actual book.

Translation: he does **not** want you treating CoT as a crystal ball. He wants you treating it as **who is already in**.

## 4. Rates are the discount rate for almost everything

“Bad news rallies”: weaker data → **lower expected path of Fed funds** → **higher** PV of stocks/bonds; gold benefits because **opportunity cost** of a 0-yield asset falls.

Use it as a **checklist** (“what did this headline do to the **rate path**?”), not as “always buy the dip.”

## 5. Some rallies are paid for

SPX back at highs with **overhead resistance** into a **catalyst gauntlet**. Separately: **manufactured** bids (Japan FX intervention, talking down war, whoever is buying bonds) **last until the payer stops**.

This is a **fragility** framework: ask **who is funding the move**.

## 6. AI bottleneck migrated off the GPU slogan

Chips still matter (Apple losing pricing power on **memory** to data-center buyers). The **macro** punchline this month is **power and the grid**, plus **electricity baked into CPI** (milk). He explicitly **rejected oil-as-AI-fuel**.

## 7. Options are a job, not a lottery ticket

Three questions **before** the chain: view, **speed**, **which risk to isolate**. 0DTE is framed as **a crowding/hedging problem that moves the index**, not as a retail toy.

## 8. Stops are liquidity, not morality

Round-number stop clusters → sweep → reverse. **Buffer by a slice of ATR past the round number.** That is **order-flow hygiene**, not a conspiracy.

## 9. Breadth so you do not invent trades

Few signals on few names → **one or two real trades a year** → people **overtrade**. Cover **pairs, calendars, breadth, funding** so dormant surfaces can wake up.

This is also a **product** argument (the letter’s multi-asset coverage). The logic is still: **no setup, no trade**.

## 10. Policy as a repeating playbook

Kalshi/NY: **regulate/tax the vice you cannot stamp out**. Look for **second-order trades** (who gets taxed, who gets the monopoly), not the lawsuit headline itself.

## 11. VIX/10 as a noise filter

Same-session TikTok: divide VIX by 10 for a rough daily SPX band; pair with a “no conviction / wandering” tape.

## 12. Who polices the bond market?

Fed vs Treasury walking apart → gold as haven if the long end has no clear cop.
